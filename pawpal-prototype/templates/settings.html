Frequently Asked Questions

How does Pawrents Self-Help work?
Imagine you are at work 3 days a week and your dog is at home alone. Your neighbour with a dog is working from home on those days! Idea: why don't we alternate hanging out with our dogs? How it works: You can set your availability to help watch other dogs, and request help when you need someone to watch yours. It's a trust-based community of dog lovers helping each other!
Is PawPal free to use?
Yes! Pawrents Self-Help is completely free. It's a community-based platform where members help each other. There are no fees or subscriptions.
How do I book a dog sitter?
Browse available neighbours, find a time slot that works for you, and send a booking request. The helper will review your request and can accept or decline. Once accepted, you can message each other to coordinate details.
How do I become a helper?
Go to "My Availability" and add time slots when you're free to help. Other members can then see your availability and send you booking requests.
